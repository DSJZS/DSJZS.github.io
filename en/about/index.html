I am a Hubei native who, for various reasons, has moved around China a lot, playing the role of an NPC in different cities. As a junior programmer who has only recently entered the field, I am mainly active on GitHub and BiliBili.

I do not have many hobbies. The few hobbies I can name are probably listening to music and playing card, board, and management games. After entering university, I almost stopped watching movies, TV shows, and entertainment programs, but I listen to a lot of music while coding and writing, and I also enjoy reading lyrics. I sometimes sing a little or play instruments, though my ability is limited. After work or during holidays, I also play games such as Yu-Gi-Oh! (mainly MASTER DUEL), RimWorld, and Minecraft.

In short, I will update this blog irregularly with various miscellaneous posts, mainly to record what I have learned, my thoughts on certain topics, and some TODO items.

All text I write on this blog may be reproduced as long as the terms of the CC license are followed.

Site Architecture

This site currently uses Hugo as the backend system. For the first half year or so after the site was created, it used the hugo-stack theme. Since July 2026, it has switched to the hugo-paged theme, with a few adjustments made for practical needs. For the best reading experience, I strongly recommend installing Source Han Serif.

This site uses the Giscus comment system, so a GitHub account is required to comment. Due to some unexpected issues, however, comments before July 2026 appear to have been lost.

For internationalization, the site currently supports only Chinese and English. In practice, Chinese remains the primary language, since I do not have enough time to translate and proofread all English content.

Finally, the site avatar comes from the MV for Fei Yu Zhuan Shen by the band Chinese Football: BiliBili link.
